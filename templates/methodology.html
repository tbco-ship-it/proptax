Data & method

Source. U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey {{ source.vintage }} 5-year estimates, table-based summary files (www2.census.gov), downloaded {{ source.fetched }}. Tables: B25103 (median real estate taxes paid, by mortgage status), B25077 (median value of owner-occupied units), B25064 (median gross rent), B19013 (median household income), B25003 (tenure), B01003 (population). Public domain.

Effective rate. B25103 median ÷ B25077 median × 100, per county and per state. This is a ratio of two medians, not the median of household ratios; it tracks published effective rates closely and is the same construction most rate comparisons use. It reflects taxes people actually paid, including exemptions and assessment caps.

Topcodes. The Census reports taxes of $200 or less as $200 and $10,000 or more as $10,000. Counties at the $200 floor are marked "or less"; rankings exclude counties under {{ '{:,}'.format(MIN_POP_RANK) }} residents where a few responses move the median.

Due dates. From state statutes and state revenue departments, one row per state, checked by hand with the date noted. Counties that set their own calendar are flagged rather than guessed.

Refresh. ACS 5-year updates each December; the site is rebuilt when the new vintage lands.
