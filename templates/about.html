About CountyTax

Assessor sites give you a mill rate against a value nobody recognizes; real-estate portals bury the rate behind a lead form. CountyTax shows the one number that predicts your bill — the effective rate homeowners in your county actually pay — for every county in the country, with the median bill and home value behind it, an estimate for your own home, and when the bill is due in your state.
