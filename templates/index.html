"Effective rate" is the number that matters

Assessors publish mill rates against an assessed value that is often a fraction of market value, so two counties with the same mill rate can bill very differently. The effective rate here is the median real-estate tax homeowners reported paying divided by the median home value in the same county — the share of your home's value that goes to property tax each year. Due dates are set by the state, penalties by the county

Texas bills are due January 31, California splits into December 10 and April 10, Illinois counties each pick their own. Due dates by state lists installments, delinquency dates and penalties with the statute behind each.
