How this benchmark was computed

Median real estate taxes paid by owner-occupied households in {{ c.name }} ({{ c.tax_label }}) divided by the median value of those homes ({{ c.home_label }}), both from the Census Bureau's American Community Survey {{ source.vintage }} 5-year estimates. It is a ratio of two medians — a reasonable area benchmark, not the assessor's mill rate and not any one household's rate. Newly purchased homes are often reassessed to sale price, so a buyer's first bill can run above it. Data & method
