How this rate was computed

Median real estate taxes paid by owner-occupied households in {{ c.name }} ({{ c.tax_med|usd }}) divided by the median value of those homes ({{ c.home_med|usd }}), both from the Census Bureau's American Community Survey {{ source.vintage }} 5-year estimates.
